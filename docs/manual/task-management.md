## Task Management 
#### **Authors:** Maggie Bragg, Matthew Allen

This feature allows Schedulers to edit, add, and remove tasks from their schedule. 

Note: Insert Screenshot of Task Management Page Here

### Add Task Button

Clicking on the "Add Task" button will direct you to a form called "Add Task". 

Note: Insert Screenshot of Add Task Form Here

#### Submit Button

Clicking on the "Submit" button after filling out the appropriate fields of the "Add Task" form will add the task to your schedule.

### Edit Task Button

Clicking on the "Edit Task" button on an existing task will direct you to a form called "Edit Task".

Note: Insert Screenshot of Edit Task Form Here

#### Submit Button

Clicking on the "Submit" button after filling out the appropriate fields of the "Edit Task" form will update the task in your schedule.

### Delete Task Button

Clicking on the "Delete Task" button on an existing task will open a confirmation window that will ask "Are you sure you want to delete this task?". Clicking "Yes" will delete the task from your calendar. Clicking "No" will keep the task as is.
