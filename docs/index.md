# Gold Star

## Introduction
Individuals with ADHD diagnoses or ADHD-like traits often struggle with consistent routine development due to various productivity-altering traits. Routines last for short time periods, and are vulnerable to persistent Routine Adaptations leading to frustration and reduced self-efficacy. 
Gold Star is a web-based habit-building and time management platform to help users affected by ADHD. Gold Star helps organize schedules, break down tasks, and build sustainable routines. Gold Star intends to make daily routine development easier to start, maintain, and complete.
