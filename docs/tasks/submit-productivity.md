# Submitting a Daily Productivity Level
The text below is from Lab 2 Section 3 as a placeholder. For Lab 4, we must express these details in a more "User Manual"-like wording.

3.1.5.1. Introduction/Purpose
Adaptive Scheduling allows Schedulers to adjust their daily schedules when unexpected events
occur, or when they are unable to complete planned tasks. Unfinished tasks can be rescheduled
to another date, allowing Schedulers to maintain flexibility without penalization for missed tasks.
The system also adjusts proposed daily schedules based on the Scheduler's selected productivity
level, the Scheduler's mood, and task priorities to manage daily responsibilities.

Stimulus: The Scheduler submits their daily Productivity level.
Response: The system adjusts the proposed daily schedule based on the Scheduler's selected
productivity level.
